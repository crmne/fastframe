fastframe 0.15.3 lets macOS apps keep working with their window closed and reopen it from the Dock, without adding a menu-bar icon. It also includes the dependency updates since 0.15.2.

Move every fastframe crate to `tag = "v0.15.3"`. Existing apps need no code changes. Apps adopting Dock-only background operation use `fastframe_tray::Dock`, call `attach()` after creating their first window, drain its events and use `fastframe_tray::idle` while no window exists.

## New

- **Reopen from the Dock without a menu-bar item.** `Dock` reports `Event::Show` when the person clicks the app's Dock icon. It is inert on other platforms, where apps can keep using `Tray`. By @crmne, for Solco's background analysis.

## Fixed

- **Updated runtime dependencies.** Includes Tokio 1.53.3 and updates to the tray, file-watching, memory-mapping and platform crates. By @crmne; thanks to Renovate for the update reports.
