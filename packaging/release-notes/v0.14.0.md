fastframe 0.14.0 lets apps name themselves in the system's volume mixer. On PipeWire and PulseAudio, an app's audio now shows its own name, icon and stream name instead of cpal's `cpal-playback-<pid>` and `cpal-pulseaudio-<pid>`, and a stream can say what it carries, such as music or a call.

Upgrade by moving every fastframe crate to the tag:

```toml
fastframe-audio = { git = "https://github.com/crmne/fastframe", tag = "v0.14.0" }
```

**Apps that also get cpal 0.18 from crates.io must patch it to fastframe's fork.** No cpal release can name streams yet, so `fastframe-audio` now gets cpal from `crmne/cpal` at `fcd8b6e`: cpal 0.18.2 with [RustAudio/cpal#1409](https://github.com/RustAudio/cpal/pull/1409), carried until a cpal release includes it. An app that also gets cpal 0.18 from crates.io, through kira or directly, adds this at its root, so it builds one cpal instead of two:

```toml
[patch.crates-io]
cpal = { git = "https://github.com/crmne/cpal", rev = "fcd8b6ebee9d822426fa8d6839cdd89af6e0d491" }
```

**`OutputOptions` and `InputOptions` literals get two fields.** A literal without `..Default::default()` adds `media_name: None, role: None`. Apps that set neither, and do not call `set_app_info`, see their streams named as before. [MIGRATION.md](https://github.com/crmne/fastframe/blob/v0.14.0/MIGRATION.md#fastframe-audio-014) has the details.

## New

- **Name the app and its streams in the volume mixer (`set_app_info`, `OutputOptions::media_name`, `OutputOptions::role`, and the same on `InputOptions`).** Call `set_app_info` once at startup with the app's name and freedesktop icon name, and every output and input the process opens afterwards carries them. A stream takes the app's name unless its options give one of its own, and `role` (`StreamRole::Music`, `Communication`, `Notification` and others) tells the sound server what it carries: WirePlumber can lower other streams during a `Communication` stream, as ZapFast's calls will use. On PipeWire they become the stream's `application.name`, `application.icon-name`, `media.name` and `media.role`, and the app's name and icon its client's. On PulseAudio the app's name names the client and the stream's proplist carries the rest. The connection kept to PipeWire's PulseAudio server for device lookups, which 0.13.1 left as `cpal-pulseaudio-<pid>` in the client list, carries the app's name too. Checked on PipeWire 1.6.9 with `pw-dump` and, through `pipewire-pulse`, with `pactl list sink-inputs`, `source-outputs` and `clients`, on both cpal's PipeWire and PulseAudio hosts, with the `idle` and `capture` examples' new `--app`, `--icon`, `--media-name` and `--role` flags. By @crmne, with the cpal change proposed upstream in [RustAudio/cpal#1409](https://github.com/RustAudio/cpal/pull/1409); thanks to the Spotifast session for asking for it.

## Known limitations

- **A stream keeps its name while it is open.** cpal cannot rename an open stream, so a player cannot show the song playing as its stream's name; the name changes only when the stream reopens. PipeWire and PulseAudio can both rename a stream, so this can come once cpal can.
- **A role shares its remembered volume.** The sound server remembers the volume of a stream with a role by its role, so every app playing `Music` shares one remembered volume; a stream without a role, the default, has its volume remembered for its app.
- **ALSA, CoreAudio and WASAPI show no names from fastframe.** ALSA has no place for them; CoreAudio and WASAPI show the executable's own name and icon.
- **PulseAudio shows the icon on the stream, not the client.** pulseaudio-rs names a client but cannot give it an icon, so on PulseAudio the icon is only in each stream's proplist.
- The Linux audio requirements and PipeWire default-input startup delay documented in [0.12.1](https://github.com/crmne/fastframe/releases/tag/v0.12.1) still apply.
