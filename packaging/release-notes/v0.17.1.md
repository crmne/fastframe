fastframe 0.17.1 lets a macOS app's Dock icon show its badge once the app uses Notification Center, and gives the app a "Badge application icon" switch in System Settings.

Move every fastframe crate to `tag = "v0.17.1"`. Nothing in an app needs to change.

## Fixed

- **The Dock badge shows on macOS.** fastframe-notifications now asks for badges along with alerts and sounds. macOS hides the Dock badge of an app registered with Notification Center unless badges are allowed, and only offers the switch to an app that asks for it. An app whose users already answered the permission prompt asks again once per launch, without a new prompt, so the switch appears for them too. Reported in [crmne/zapfast#935](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/issues/935) by @runaway-code.
